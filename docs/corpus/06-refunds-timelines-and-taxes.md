# Refund Timelines, Taxes and Payment Methods

*Sample knowledge-base article for the Resolv-HQ prototype. Synthetic, team-authored. Version 1.0. Companion to the Customer Billing Policy; the values below are example values for the prototype, not real commercial terms.*

## 1. Refund review time
A refund request is reviewed by a billing specialist within 3 business days of being submitted with the invoice or transaction reference and the reason.

## 2. Refund payment time
If a refund is approved, the money is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Banks and mobile-money providers may take additional time to show it.

## 3. Taxes
Invoice amounts include applicable taxes, shown as a separate line on the invoice. If a tax line looks wrong, raise a billing dispute.

## 4. Payment methods
Payments can be made by card or mobile money. Payment status is shown as pending, successful, failed, or refunded.

## 5. Changing billing details
Billing name and billing address can be changed from the account settings page. Changes apply to future invoices only; past invoices are not reissued. If the address cannot be changed in settings, open a support request with category Billing.

## 6. Who approves refunds
The assistant cannot approve or process a refund. An authorized administrator approves or rejects it.
