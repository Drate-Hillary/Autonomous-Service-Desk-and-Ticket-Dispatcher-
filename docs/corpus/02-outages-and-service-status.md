# Outages and Service Status

*Sample knowledge-base article for the Resolv-HQ prototype. Synthetic, team-authored. Version 1.0.*

## 1. What counts as an outage
An outage is a period when a Resolv-HQ service (sign-in, chat, request submission, or file upload) is unavailable or failing for many customers. A problem affecting only one account is handled as a normal support request, not an outage.

## 2. Checking service status
Ask the assistant to check service status, or open the Status page in the app. Status is shown as Operational, Degraded, or Outage, with the time of the last update.

## 3. During an outage
Requests already submitted are kept and are not lost. Replies may be delayed. You do not need to submit a duplicate request; duplicates slow down resolution.

## 4. After an outage
Affected requests are reviewed by a support agent. If an outage caused a missed response time, the agent will note this on the request.

## 5. Planned maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced in the app at least 24 hours ahead and is normally scheduled outside support hours.
