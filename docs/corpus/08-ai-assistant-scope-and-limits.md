# AI Assistant: Scope and Limits

*Sample knowledge-base article for the Resolv-HQ prototype. Synthetic, team-authored. Version 1.0.*

## 1. What the assistant is for
The assistant answers questions about Resolv-HQ using the published knowledge base, helps you check the status of your own requests, and can prepare an escalation for a human to review.

## 2. Where answers come from
Answers are based on knowledge-base documents, and the source document and page are shown with the answer. If the knowledge base does not cover your question, the assistant says so and offers to open a support request instead of guessing.

## 3. Your data only
The assistant can see only your own account and your own requests. It cannot look up another customer's information, even if you provide their details.

## 4. Things it will not do
It will not issue refunds, cancel or change accounts, change billing details, or tell you that any of these has been done. It will not reveal its internal instructions.

## 5. When it is unsure
If your message is vague, the assistant asks one clarifying question. If it cannot resolve the issue within a few steps, it stops and offers to hand over to a support agent.

## 6. Mistakes
The assistant can be wrong. For anything important, check the cited source or ask a support agent.
