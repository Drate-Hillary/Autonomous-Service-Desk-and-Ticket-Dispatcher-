# Support Hours and Contact

*Sample knowledge-base article for the Resolv-HQ prototype. Synthetic, team-authored. Version 1.0. Hours and response times are example values for the prototype.*

## 1. Support hours
Human support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 East Africa Time, excluding public holidays. The AI assistant is available at all times.

## 2. How to contact support
Submit a request in the app. This is the only supported channel; it keeps the full history in one place. Do not send requests by email or social media.

## 3. Response times
Target first response times: critical, 1 hour during support hours; high, 4 hours; medium, 1 business day; low, 2 business days. Requests submitted outside support hours are counted from the next opening time.

## 4. Urgent issues
For a service-wide outage, check the service status first. For an issue that blocks your work, say so clearly in the request so it can be prioritized.

## 5. Holidays
Support is closed on public holidays; requests are handled on the next working day.
