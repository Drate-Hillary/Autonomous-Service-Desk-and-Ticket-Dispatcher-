# Escalation and Approvals

*Sample knowledge-base article for the Resolv-HQ prototype. Synthetic, team-authored. Version 1.0.*

## 1. When a request is escalated
A request is escalated when it needs a decision or action the AI assistant is not allowed to take, when the knowledge base does not cover the question, or when the customer asks for a person.

## 2. What the AI assistant can do
The assistant can search the knowledge base, look up the customer's own account status and requests, check service status, and draft an escalation ticket. A drafted ticket is only a draft; it is not filed until a staff member approves it.

## 3. What the AI assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot issue refunds, cancel or change an account, change billing details, or confirm that any such action has been completed. These actions always require a human decision.

## 4. The approval queue
Drafted escalations and any action that needs authorization are placed in the approval queue. An authorized administrator reviews the evidence and approves or rejects the item. The decision and the reviewer are recorded.

## 5. What the customer sees
The customer is told that the request has been sent for review and receives the outcome on the request. The assistant never says an action is done before it has been approved.
